As the semester entered its final days, all three major labs - **Digital Logic Design (DLD), Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), and Database Systems** - had been successfully completed. Our project exhibitions had been conducted, project submissions were finalized, lab manuals had been submitted, and all practical evaluations were over. At this stage, students were mainly waiting for the final lab results and preparing for the upcoming final examinations. During this period, our DLD instructor shared the lab result sheet in the class WhatsApp group. The total marks for the DLD lab were 100, and Alhamdulillah, I secured **88 marks out of 100**. Seeing this result made me very happy because it reflected the hard work and consistency that I had maintained throughout the semester. My group members also achieved excellent results, both scoring above 80 marks. This made our entire group proud because we had worked together sincerely in every lab session, learned new concepts, designed circuits, implemented them through simulation software, and finally tested them on breadboards. What made this subject special for me was that I never studied it only for grades. Instead, I genuinely enjoyed understanding the concepts, exploring their practical applications, and connecting theory with real-world hardware implementations. Receiving a strong result in the lab was therefore not only an academic achievement but also a reward for the passion and effort I had invested throughout the semester.

Later that same evening, an interesting and meaningful experience took place in our hostel. A group of senior students, along with students from other universities who were involved in religious outreach activities, visited our hostel to meet students and share a message about the importance of faith and worship. They visited different rooms, and eventually came to our room where my roommates and I were busy with our studies and personal work. We welcomed them warmly, offered them water, and invited them to sit with us. They requested only a few minutes of our time, and soon we were all sitting together in a circle. During the discussion, they asked about our studies, university life, and daily routines before sharing reminders about the importance of prayer, discipline, and maintaining a strong connection with Allah. Their primary focus was on the significance of offering prayers regularly and making them a consistent part of daily life. The discussion encouraged many students to reflect on their habits and priorities. Personally, I have always believed that success comes from hard work combined with the blessings and guidance of Allah. Throughout my academic journey, whether in matriculation, my first semester, or my second semester, I have considered faith, prayer, and the prayers of my parents to be among the most important factors behind every achievement. After the session, my roommates and I discussed the message further and renewed our commitment to offer prayers more regularly in congregation whenever possible. It was a simple but powerful reminder during an important stage of our university life.

As the final examinations moved closer, the atmosphere across the hostel and campus became noticeably different. Since it was the last week before exams, every student had a unique approach to preparation. Some students felt stressed because they had not been consistent throughout the semester, while others were relatively relaxed because they had managed their studies steadily from the beginning. Alhamdulillah, I belonged to the second group. My habit throughout the semester was to prepare topics gradually and revise them before examinations rather than leaving everything for the last moment. Because of this approach, I felt more confident and focused during the revision period. Even my roommate often reminded me that most of my preparation had already been completed and that I mainly needed revision. During these days, students frequently exchanged notes, discussed important topics, and helped one another with difficult concepts. Some of my friends borrowed notes that I had prepared during the semester, and I gladly shared them and explained topics whenever needed. This spirit of cooperation created a positive learning environment among students. As the final examinations approached, everyone became fully focused on their academic goals. Looking back, this period was not only about preparing for exams but also about appreciating the journey of the entire semester, the lessons learned, the friendships built, and the personal growth achieved through both academic and life experiences.
