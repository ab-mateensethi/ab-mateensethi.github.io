As the quiz week began, students faced an unexpected challenge that affected our daily academic activities. We had quizzes scheduled for two different subjects, but at the same time, the university was experiencing serious internet connectivity issues. During my first semester, I had rarely faced such problems, but in the second semester, network disruptions became more frequent. This particular week was especially difficult because the internet would sometimes remain unavailable for three to four hours at a time. Since university life requires constant access to online resources for projects, assignments, lectures and research, this issue became a major concern for many students. Understanding the impact it could have on our studies, especially with final examinations approaching, we contacted the university management and submitted an application requesting a solution. As students, we knew that maintaining the quality of our education was important, so we decided to raise the issue responsibly while continuing our studies with patience and determination.

Amid these challenges, we also received the results of our Statistics midterm examination. Our Statistics instructor, Sir Fazal, reviewed the papers and discussed the overall performance of the class. Personally, I was slightly concerned about my result because I had missed a few lectures due to other important academic responsibilities. As a result, some topics were not completely clear to me before the examination, although I had tried to understand them with the help of my friends. Alhamdulillah, when the papers were distributed, I was happy to see that I had secured **23 out of 30 marks**. While there was still room for improvement, I considered it a good result. After receiving my paper, I approached my instructor and carefully discussed the mistakes I had made. He explained the concepts that I had misunderstood and this helped me clear my confusion and strengthen my understanding of those topics. This experience reminded me that learning from mistakes is just as important as achieving good marks.

On the same day, after the break, we had our **Database Systems quiz**. Despite the ongoing internet problems, I had prepared myself well in advance. Whenever the internet became available, I used the opportunity to download lecture materials, create notes and practice important concepts. In fact, the day before the quiz, the internet remained unavailable for most of the day, but thankfully I had already completed my preparation. When I entered the classroom, our instructor arranged the students according to their roll numbers and the quiz began. The quiz focused on **MySQL Queries and Relational Algebra**, two important topics in database systems. Before attempting the questions, I carefully read and understood each one and then solved them confidently. Alhamdulillah, the quiz went well and I felt satisfied with my performance. Looking back, this week taught me an important lesson: academic success is not only about having ideal conditions but also about adapting to challenges, staying consistent and making the best use of available resources. Despite the difficulties, I remained focused on my goals and continued moving forward in my academic journey.
