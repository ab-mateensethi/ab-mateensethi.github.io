As the final examination week continued, another encouraging achievement came when the result of our Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) lab was officially announced. Our lab instructor, Dr. Rimsha Chaudhry, first shared separate evaluation sheets on Google Classroom, including project marks, viva marks and overall lab performance. A few days later, the complete result out of one hundred marks was uploaded. Alhamdulillah, I secured **89.9 marks out of 100**, which was a proud moment for me because it reflected my hard work throughout the semester. OOP quickly became one of my favorite subjects, even though it was my first formal programming language. The way Dr. Akram explained programming concepts step by step in theory classes and how we implemented those concepts in the lab made learning both enjoyable and effective. The projects we completed during the semester further strengthened my understanding and increased my interest in software development. Although the final grading was still pending, I was very satisfied with the result and motivated to continue improving my programming skills in future semesters.

Along with this academic success, I received another piece of good news that made this period even more memorable. Earlier in the semester, I had applied for the **HEC Coursera Program** and although I had already been selected, I was still waiting for my license activation. During the examination days, Alhamdulillah, I finally received my Coursera license and successfully activated my account. Coursera is a globally recognized online learning platform that offers professional courses and certifications from leading universities and organizations around the world. The license was valid until December 2026 and allowed me to enroll in a wide range of courses free of cost. Since I have always been interested in learning new technologies and improving my technical skills, receiving this opportunity made me very happy. With summer vacations approaching, I started planning how I would use this platform to learn programming, explore new technologies, work on practical projects and earn valuable certifications. I viewed this achievement not only as a reward but also as a responsibility to make productive use of the opportunity provided by HEC.

Meanwhile, the final examination week was progressing steadily. Our examination schedule was quite busy because most papers were held on consecutive days, with only a few gaps between certain subjects. As a result, effective time management became extremely important. After attempting each paper, I would take a short rest before immediately beginning preparation for the next exam. Group study sessions with my roommates and classmates also became a regular part of our routine. Whenever someone faced difficulty understanding a topic, we would discuss it together, share notes and help one another prepare more effectively. I shared my lecture notes with several friends and together we revised important concepts and practiced potential examination questions. These collaborative study sessions not only improved our preparation but also strengthened our teamwork and friendship. Overall, this phase of the semester was a combination of academic achievement, exciting new opportunities and focused preparation for final examinations. As I moved closer to the completion of my second semester, I felt grateful for everything I had learned and optimistic about the journey ahead.
