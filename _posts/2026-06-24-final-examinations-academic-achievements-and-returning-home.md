Alhamdulillah, by this time all of my second-semester final examinations had been completed successfully. The examination week had passed and I was very satisfied with my performance because I had prepared well throughout the semester and revised all important concepts before each paper. As a result, most of the exams felt manageable and I was able to attempt them with confidence. After every exam, I discussed the papers with my friends and classmates. Some students were extremely happy with their performance, while others felt that a few papers could have gone better. However, almost everyone was relieved because the examinations were finally over and the long summer vacation was about to begin. This feeling of relief and accomplishment created a positive atmosphere among the students. During the same period, another piece of good news arrived when our Digital Logic Design (DLD) theory result was announced. Our instructor shared the marks sheet in the WhatsApp group and Alhamdulillah, I secured **82.5% marks** in the subject. Earlier in the semester, my performance in some quizzes and the midterm examination had not been exactly as I wanted, but instead of becoming disappointed, I treated those results as opportunities to learn. I consulted my instructor, discussed improvement strategies with seniors and worked harder on my weak areas. The effort paid off when I scored **35 out of 40 marks in the final examination**, which clearly reflected my improvement and growth throughout the semester.

Alongside the DLD result, we also received updates regarding our Database Systems course. A day before our last examination, our respected instructor, Dr. Bilal Ahmad, informed us that the final papers would be discussed after the last exam. Therefore, when our final paper ended on Monday morning, students from both sections gathered in the classroom to review their Database papers. The instructor carefully explained each question, discussed common mistakes and showed the marking distribution for every section of the paper. Alhamdulillah, I was delighted to see that I had secured **35 out of 40 marks** in the Database final examination. Since the paper was considered challenging by most students, only a few students from both sections managed to score above thirty marks. By the grace of Allah, I achieved the **second-highest marks across both sections**, which was a proud and memorable achievement for me. More importantly, the experience reinforced an important lesson that consistent preparation, regular revision and learning from mistakes can lead to meaningful improvement. Receiving these results before leaving the university gave me additional motivation and confidence for the future.

After checking the papers and completing all academic responsibilities, the moment finally arrived to leave the hostel and return home. Back in my room, I carefully packed my belongings, pressed my clothes, organized my books and prepared my luggage for the journey. It was an emotional experience because the academic year had passed so quickly. During this time, I had built strong friendships, learned valuable lessons from my teachers, collaborated with talented classmates and gained guidance from supportive seniors. While I felt sad to leave behind the hostel environment and the people who had become an important part of my daily life, I was also excited because I would soon reunite with my family after a long period away from home. One by one, my friends and roommates departed for their hometowns, exchanging good wishes and prayers for the future. Finally, after saying farewell to my friends, teachers, roommates and university life for the semester, I also began my journey home. Upon reaching home, I shared my achievements and examination experiences with my family, who had supported me throughout the semester. Now, with the semester completed, I was eagerly waiting for the official final result and GPA announcement. I had full trust in Allah and confidence in the effort I had put into my studies. Regardless of the final numbers, I felt grateful for everything I had learned during the semester and optimistic about the opportunities that awaited me in the future.
