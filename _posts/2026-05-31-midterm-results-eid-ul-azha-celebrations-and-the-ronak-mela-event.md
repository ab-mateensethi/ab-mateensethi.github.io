As the following week began, different teachers started discussing and returning the midterm examination papers. Among them, our Database Systems instructor, Dr. Bilal Ahmad, reviewed our papers in a very organized and educational manner. Before distributing the papers, he explained the complete marking criteria on the board and showed how marks had been allocated for each question and subpart. He also discussed common mistakes, particularly in the ER Diagram section, and guided students on how they could improve their understanding of database concepts. After the discussion, the papers were distributed. Alhamdulillah, I secured **24 out of 30 marks** in the Database Systems midterm examination. Overall, the class result was satisfactory, although some students had concerns regarding their marks. Dr. Bilal Ahmad encouraged everyone to discuss any issue openly, and several students successfully clarified their concerns and even received revised marks after rechecking. For me, this result was a valuable learning experience because I identified my mistakes and became more determined to improve. Since the entire midterm syllabus would also be included in the final examination, I decided to focus on strengthening the concepts where I had lost marks.

Just two days later, the excitement of **Eid-ul-Azha holidays** began to build. We were informed that the university would remain closed for one week, and I was extremely happy because I had not visited home for quite some time. During phone calls, my family often told me how much they missed me, and I felt the same way. They were especially waiting for my arrival because every year, my elder brother and I visit the local cattle market together to purchase an animal for Qurbani. As soon as the holidays started, I said goodbye to my roommates and friends and traveled back home. The next day, my brother and I went to the cattle market and selected a healthy goat for Eid. On the first day of Eid-ul-Azha, we performed the sacrifice and prayed to Allah Almighty to accept our Qurbani and bless our family. The holiday was filled with joy, family gatherings and memorable moments. I spent quality time with relatives and friends, enjoyed the festive atmosphere and felt refreshed after a busy academic period. As the holidays gradually came to an end, I purchased the necessary items from the market and started preparing for my return to university.

With only a few days remaining before classes resumed, I packed my belongings, received prayers and best wishes from my parents, and set off once again for university. Upon returning, I learned that a special event called **Ronak Mela** was scheduled for the very next day. Preparations for the event had actually started before the Eid holidays, when students purchased stalls through a bidding process. Different groups planned various activities and businesses for the event. The next morning, organizers arranged all the stalls on the ground outside the Computer Science Department, and the event officially began at 10:00 AM. After finishing my classes, I visited the venue with my friends and was impressed by the variety of stalls. There were food stalls, gaming stalls, cosmetic stalls and many other creative setups managed by students. One of my roommates had also established a Gol Gappy stall with his classmates, so naturally I visited their stall and enjoyed the food with my friends. We spent time exploring different stalls, supporting fellow students and enjoying the lively atmosphere. The event not only provided entertainment but also highlighted the creativity, teamwork and entrepreneurial spirit of the students. Looking back, this period was a wonderful combination of academic learning, family bonding, spiritual reflection and campus life experiences that made my university journey even more memorable.
