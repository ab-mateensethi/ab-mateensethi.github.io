As a Computer Engineering student at UET Lahore Faisalabad Campus, I created this GitHub portfolio website to document my academic journey, learning experiences, challenges and achievements throughout my degree. This initiative was inspired by Dr. Bilal Ahmad, who encouraged us to build a meaningful digital footprint instead of limiting our work to classroom assignments and examinations. Dr. Bilal Ahmad is not only our instructor for Programming Fundamentals and Database Systems but also an expert in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL). His research and practical work focus on developing machine learning solutions for real-world problems, particularly in domains where data quality and accuracy are critical. One of his consistent recommendations to students is to work with medical datasets because they are usually collected with high precision and can contribute to solving meaningful problems that benefit society. Following his guidance, I decided to maintain this blog as a record of my growth as an engineering student while improving my technical writing and professional communication skills. Those interested in his academic and professional work can explore his LinkedIn profile at [https://www.linkedin.com/in/drbilalphd/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/drbilalphd/), his Google Scholar profile at [https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en) and his Facebook page at [https://www.facebook.com/Dr.BilalAhm](https://www.facebook.com/Dr.BilalAhm). Through this portfolio, I hope to share my experiences, lessons learned and technical development throughout my studies at UET Lahore Faisalabad Campus.

Traveling from my hometown of Dina to Faisalabad for university admission was an experience full of excitement and anticipation. The journey gave me time to reflect on the new phase of life I was about to enter. Upon reaching the university, I first explored the campus and checked into my assigned hostel. The hostel room was small but comfortable and it quickly became my personal space for study and rest. Walking around the campus, I noticed the lively environment with students moving between classes, creating an atmosphere that felt both motivating and welcoming.

Settling into hostel life involved more than just arranging my belongings. I met my roommates, who were equally eager to adjust to this new chapter. Our first conversations were simple introductions, but they quickly turned into discussions about university life, hostel routines and shared interests. Understanding hostel rules, daily schedules and the responsibilities that came with communal living was initially challenging, yet it provided an opportunity to grow independently. Experiencing this combination of freedom and responsibility gave me a sense of maturity and prepared me for the demands of university life.

Overall, the first day was a mixture of excitement, nervousness and curiosity. From the travel experience to the initial impressions of the campus and hostel, every moment contributed to a feeling of stepping into a world of new possibilities. Meeting new people, exploring the environment and starting to adapt to routines marked the beginning of an important journey that would shape my university life.
