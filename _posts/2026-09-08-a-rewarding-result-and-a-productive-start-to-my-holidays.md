After the completion of my second-semester final examinations, my holidays officially began. I wanted to use this break not only for entertainment but also for improving my health, refreshing my mind and learning new skills. Therefore, one of the first things I did was join a gym in my city. I strongly believe that along with education, physical health is also important because a healthy body helps us maintain a fresh and active mind. Many students spend most of their holidays playing mobile games or staying at home for long hours, but I wanted to create a healthier routine for myself. I decided to go to the gym in the morning after offering Fajr prayer. The gym opened at 5:00 AM, so I usually went there around that time and spent about one to one and a half hours exercising. The peaceful morning environment and physical activity helped me feel refreshed and energetic for the rest of the day. After returning home, I had my post-workout meal, took some rest and then continued with my other daily activities. In this way, I tried to make my holidays balanced by giving time to both my health and personal development.

While I was enjoying my holidays and following this new routine, I was also waiting for the most important academic update: my second-semester result. After about three weeks of holidays, the results of all our subjects were gradually completed, as different teachers shared their marks and result sheets in the class groups. Finally, the complete result was uploaded on the LMS. On the day of the result, I was excited and a little nervous at the same time. Before checking it, I sincerely prayed to Allah Almighty and hoped that the hard work I had done throughout the semester would bring a positive outcome. When I logged into the LMS and saw my grades, I felt extremely happy and proud to see that I had achieved a 3.82 GPA out of 4.00 in my second semester. This was a very meaningful achievement for me because it reflected the effort, consistency and lessons I had gained throughout the semester. I immediately shared the result with my parents and they were very happy and congratulated me. After calculating my overall CGPA, I achieved a 3.72 CGPA. In my first semester, when I was new to university life and had very little experience of how everything worked, I had achieved a 3.60 GPA. In the second semester, I learned from the mistakes I had made during my first semester, improved my study habits and worked harder, which helped me raise my GPA to 3.82, equivalent to 95.5% of the 4.00 scale. After receiving this result, I offered two Nafl prayers of gratitude along with my Zuhr prayer and thanked Allah Almighty for blessing me with the reward of my efforts. This result gave me even more motivation to continue working hard with the same dedication in my upcoming semesters.

With the happiness of my result, I also became more determined to make my holidays productive. Since I had already received my Coursera license through HEC, I decided to use this opportunity to learn new skills and strengthen the knowledge I had gained during my first two semesters. I prepared a list of my learning priorities, including areas such as web development, app development, artificial intelligence and other technical skills. Even in areas where I had already completed university projects, I wanted to explore them further and develop a deeper understanding. I created a daily timetable and reserved around two hours for learning through Coursera. One of the first short courses I completed was AI for Brainstorming and Planning, where I learned about AI technology and effective AI prompting. I then explored Introduction to Generative AI, which further increased my understanding of modern AI tools and technologies. Since I had already worked with Machine Learning during my first semester and had also applied it in my second-semester Database project, I decided to explore Machine Learning more deeply. I started a longer Machine Learning course and completed quizzes and projects that helped me strengthen my practical understanding of the field. I was happy to earn certificates for these courses and I have attached them to my portfolio as a record of this learning journey. Alongside my studies and skill development, I also visited my village during the holidays because I wanted to enjoy my break and spend quality time with family while still using my time positively. Overall, this phase of my holidays became a combination of health, achievement, learning and enjoyment and I was excited to continue this journey with the same passion and consistency.
